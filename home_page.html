What is EDU AI?

EDU AI is an intelligent e-learning system that transforms educational materials into interactive quizzes and personalized learning paths. Using Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning, it extracts key concepts from your notes or documents and generates relevant questions in multiple formats, saving time and delivering tailored experiences for each learner.

Our Mission at EDU AI

At EDU AI, we believe that education shouldn’t be one-size-fits-all. That’s why we’ve created an intelligent platform that turns static documents into living, breathing learning journeys. We empower educators to teach more and type less. We help learners focus on what matters — mastering knowledge through smart, adaptive quizzes.

From real-time feedback to personalized learning paths, everything we build is rooted in one mission: to make digital education smarter, faster, and human-centric.
